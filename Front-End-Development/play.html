Building Faith in our Society

            

            

                

                    God's 2 most imporant commands are: love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, soul, strength and mind, and love thy neighbor as thyself.
                    Jesus Christ also teaches us to "treat others the way we want to be treated." One of the most important things which are lacking in the world today is love. People steal, cheat, covet, bruise, hurt, and yet they wouldn't want anyone else doing those exact same things back to them. The Bible isn't a rule book, it's a love book that keeps people safe from evil. No wonder Jesus said, "On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets."
                    God is a God of love and unity. And because he is a God of love, he is also a God of justice. He punishes those who do evil to others, and blesses and rewards those who do good. And yes, sometimes God does allow suffering, he wants to give those evil people time to change, before he punishes them. And sometimes God allows suffering because he wants people to learn to be thankful for what they have. When was the last time you said thank you to God for having a roof over your head, good clothes to wear, and two legs to walk. Not everyone has those things, not everyone is privileged to have the little that many take for granted.
                    Jesus Christ wants us to learn to love, not hate, he wants to learn how to bind, not bruise. He wants us to learn to help, not abandon. God is a God of love, and he is coming back for a glorious church without spots and wrinkles. He sees when people are hurt, and when people do them hurt. Some day he will judge the world. God is calling the world to repetance, "repent for the kingdom of heaven is at hand." Some day he will judge all people in truth, righteousness and justice. Jesus Christ wants us to repent of our sins so that we will not partake of his great wrath upon those who are evil and disobedient. He says, "Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me. " - Revelation 3:20, please don't ignore Jesus' call today. He died for our sins and wants us to turn to him and stop doing evil.
                    Now here's a short story before we round up, "there once was a hero in the city named Charlie. He did lots of good things, he gave money to the poor, he always helped the old lady across the street, and he always stood up for those who had been wrongly accused. He went to church every Sunday and always desired to walk as Jesus walked and live as Jesus lived. He was a good man, and everyone looked up to him. But one day, he did something bad, really bad. And he stood before the Judge. And the Judge passed a sentence.
                    The Judge asked Charlie, "any final words?"
                    Charlie replied and said, "good sir! You know, I think I've done a lot of good things in my life, do you think you could let me off the hook just this once."
                    "I know Charlie, you're a good man, but if I let you off the hook, that won't be fair. You did something bad, and justice has to be served."
                    Fortunately for us, that's not what God does. Yes he is the Judge, but instead of sending his great wrath upon us, he sent his son Jesus to give us a second chance, he waits patiently, to see whether we will change, whether we will turn from our wicked ways. He waits to see if humans will realize that they're wrong, they've hurt many, they lied to many, they've stole from many, they've bruised many, they've been unfair, they've cheated, they've wrongfully oppressed innocent people, and shed blood. God doesnt' have pleasure in the death of the wicked but wants to see whether they will repent and change. (2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23)
                    And that what makes God different from the judges of the Earth, he gives us many chances to make things right, but if we refuse and rebel, and are stubborn, then he has to and will render Justice. He doesn't want people to suffer in Hell. But after sending so many prophets, pastors, teachers, and scribes, (Matthew 23:34; Jeremiah 7:25-26) whom many people have refused to hear, then suddenly destruction comes. (1 Thessalonians 5:3)
                    The Bible says that, "wisdom cries aloud in the streets and she raises her voice in the open field" but people refuse to hear! (Proverbs 1:20-33). God is going to continue to be mercifully and show us abundant grace, but let's not take his mercy and grace for granted. Let's stop refusing him, and start obeying him and let's turn to Jesus for the remission of sins and live a holy life that we may escape the Great Wrath that is soon to come. 
                    Jesus is coming back soon, and he wants everyone to have to the chance to repent and turn away, he is talking to you and to me today, "repent!"
                    Revelation 22: 20-21 "He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen."
