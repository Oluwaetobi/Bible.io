About Bible.io

Bible.io is an online massive global Christian game where you can compete with users around the world as well as learn and study the Bible, read Christian articles, watch Christian videos, and much more, our goal and vision is to make the Bible fun for kids, as well as give kids an oppurtunity to compete with one another. It's online, it's global, it's fun, it's Bible.io

Bible.io was first founded in the year 2025, August 24. It was an exciting year for Bible.io, it was originally built with block-coding, a block-based visual programming language, built on Scratch. Later it became so heavy that it had to be run and packaged with Turbowarp to run in JavaScript. But even with that, problems arose. The game became so heavy that by the year 2026 it would take anyone from 1 to 15 minutes to load the game depending on how fast the user's computer was. By the month of August, in the year 2026, omoOlorun1 had enough. So we started redeveloping the game in an entirely new environment with languages that actually drove the internet. Not with any particular engine built by anyone but core solid languages that built that internet itself. Such as HTML/CSS and JavaScript.

Our goal is to make the Bible fun and interesting for kids and peopel of all ages. Lots of kids these days find the Bible boring, and would rather play more stimulating things such as games, well hear at Bible.io we do both, we've turned the Bible into a fun interactive Website, not just limited to games, but videos, articles, you can even design your own customizable bibletar to represent you in online global competitions around the world. Bible.io is meant to be fun, engage not just kids, but teens, adults, people across all ages and ethnicities. We hope you have fun here at Bible.io and remember, it's online, it's massive, it's global, it's fun, it's Bible.io

We hope to reach millions in fact billions around the world, with our FREE and FUN, multiplayer game and Website. Remember, Jesus loves you and he died to save you from your sins, and he wants to see you with Him in heaven. God bless you all, and stay blessed!
